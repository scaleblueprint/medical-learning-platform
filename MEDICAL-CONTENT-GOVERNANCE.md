# Medical Content Governance

## Roles
- Product/learning team: interaction design, explanation structure, accessibility, platform behaviour.
- Medical faculty: accuracy, curriculum mapping, terminology and final approval.
- Medical students: clarity, cognitive load, usefulness and misconception feedback.

## Publishing states
1. Draft
2. Student reviewed
3. Faculty reviewed
4. Approved for prototype sharing

The current build intentionally stops at Draft / faculty-review-required.

## Boundaries
The learning environment should explain approved educational concepts and generate practice from approved source material. It should not provide patient-specific diagnosis or treatment recommendations.
