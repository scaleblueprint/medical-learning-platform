# Therapeutic Area Learning Standard

Therapeutic-area journeys are an **additional navigation and integration view** over the MBBS curriculum. They do not replace subject teaching, professional-year structure or official competency mapping.

## Purpose
A therapeutic-area route answers a different student question:

> How does one condition evolve across the subjects and phases in which I learn it?

The first prototype is **Diabetes through MBBS**.

## Non-negotiable structure
Every therapeutic-area journey must:
1. Preserve MBBS phase order.
2. Identify the professional phase/year for every node.
3. Identify the subject responsible for that learning lens.
4. Distinguish foundation, core disease/treatment and integrated clinical lenses.
5. Show what came before, what the current subject adds and where the concept returns later.
6. Keep exact competency codes only when verified against the current official curriculum.
7. Mark all unverified competency mappings as pending faculty verification.
8. Keep prototype medical teaching material `faculty-review-required` until approved.

## Topic design contract
Each syllabus lens should contain:
- a student-facing question;
- at least three subject-specific concepts;
- a simple visual sequence;
- an analogy or mental model;
- a direct medical mapping;
- an explicit statement of where the analogy stops;
- a compact memory anchor;
- curriculum placement and review status;
- previous/next therapeutic-area context.

## Diabetes prototype sequence
### Phase I / First Professional
- Physiology — normal glucose homeostasis and endocrine control.
- Biochemistry — carbohydrate metabolism and glucose handling.

### Phase II / Second Professional
- Pathology — diabetes mechanisms and complications.
- Pharmacology — antidiabetic pharmacology; NMC 2024 competency PH7.1 is explicitly verified.

### Phase III Part I
- Community Medicine — prevention, risk, screening and counselling as an NCD.
- Ophthalmology — diabetic retinopathy as organ-specific integration.

### Phase III Part II / Final Professional
- General Medicine — clinical integration of classification, diagnosis, complications, management principles and monitoring.
- Obstetrics & Gynaecology — gestational diabetes as a special clinical context.
- Paediatrics — childhood diabetes and developmental/family context.

## Safety and educational boundary
Therapeutic-area content is for medical learning. It must not use the prototype to diagnose a user, adjust treatment, recommend medications, set personal targets or substitute for clinical care.

## Reuse
New therapeutic areas should be registered independently from subject lessons so the same syllabus content can remain available through both:
- **Curriculum-first navigation**, and
- **Therapeutic-area navigation**.
