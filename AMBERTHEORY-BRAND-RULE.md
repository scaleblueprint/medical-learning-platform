# AmberTheory Brand Rule

For this project, whenever a requirement says **AmberTheory logo**, use the canonical rich JPEG lockup from the AmberTheory parent brand repository:

`scaleblueprint/form-and-wonder/public/ambertheory-lockup.jpg`

Canonical raw asset:
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scaleblueprint/form-and-wonder/main/public/ambertheory-lockup.jpg`

Do not substitute the visible AmberTheory logo with:
- `amber-theory-mark.svg`
- an `AT` monogram
- a text-only wordmark
- a recreated approximation
- a historical Form & Wonder or Spark & Spire asset

Technical browser icons/favicons may use a purpose-built icon, but they must not be presented or described as the AmberTheory logo.

The visible product hierarchy should be:
1. canonical AmberTheory JPEG lockup
2. product name: Medical Learning Lab
